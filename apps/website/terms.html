2. What ChromeBoost is

ChromeBoost lets an AI assistant operate your own Chrome browser: opening pages, reading page content, clicking, typing, hovering, dragging and saving files you ask for.

  • The extension and MCP server connect a coding agent you already run, such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI, to your browser. They have no server of ours behind them.
  • The desktop app wraps the same parts in a window so you can give instructions in plain English without a terminal. It runs Anthropic's Claude software on your computer under your own Claude account, and adds a paid licence.

Nothing runs on our infrastructure. Everything happens on your machine, between your browser, software you installed, and the providers named in the privacy policy.
